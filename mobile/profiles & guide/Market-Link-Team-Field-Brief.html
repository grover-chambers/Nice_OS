Seven rules

Non-negotiable · the registry is the asset
1

No pin, no record. An outlet without GPS does not count toward your day.

2

No photo, no record. The shopfront photo is how we verify the visit happened.

3

Pin at the door. Never from the matatu, the next shop, or the end of the street.

4

Closed and refused are data. Log them with the reason and revisit on Friday. Never skip silently.

5

Two pins inside 15 metres get flagged. Ian runs the check Wednesday and Friday. Your lead clears flags before Monday.

6

Leads spot-check 10% daily. Physically revisit two or three outlets and confirm the record matches the shop.

7

Everything syncs by 18:00. No offline backlog past 24 hours. A dead device is a kit problem, not a data excuse.
